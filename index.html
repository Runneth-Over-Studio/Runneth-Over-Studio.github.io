What I'm Building

Before the games comes the engine they'll run on. It's well along, and the first playable demo is next.

Gopher Wood Engine
Pre-alpha

Gopher Wood is the studio's in-house 2D game engine. It's written in C# and built on MonoGame. It isn't meant to compete with established engines. It's meant to give the studio ownership of its foundation: no licensing surprises, nothing hidden, and every system understood. It will stay focused on 2D until it has shipped real games.

Roadmap progress

13 of 16 first-milestone phases complete (as of November 2026)

Working today
  • Scene lifecycle and dependency injection
  • 2D rendering, camera, and viewport scaling
  • Input actions with keyboard and controller bindings
  • Tilemaps and layered worlds
  • Physics via Jolt
  • Entity Component System via Arch
  • Spatial audio and music
  • Save-game persistence
  • 2D lighting and particle effects
  • Content pipeline integration

Every subsystem has its own runnable test lab and automated tests, and continuous integration runs on Windows and Linux.

Up next
  • An integrated demo game: a short, complete, playable game that uses all of the engine's systems together.
  • Audio mixing: master, music, and effects volume that players can control.
  • Shader-based rendering: waiting on MonoGame's upcoming effect pipeline.
ABOUT

Why the Studio Exists

Runneth Over Studio, LLC is an independent studio founded in 2024. Its name comes from Psalm 23:5: “my cup runneth over.”

Many players feel underserved by today's games. Too often, mature games rely on glorifying objectionable themes, while many Christian games are limited by a Sunday school aesthetic. Runneth Over Studio exists to make games that refuse that binary.

The studio draws on the long tradition of Christian art (evocative, bold, and enduring) to explore deep themes and allegory through compelling gameplay. The goal is games that resonate both creatively and spiritually, with the faith woven into the work rather than preached at the player, and without compromising on fun or depth.

That's also why the studio builds on open tools and its own engine. Work meant to last shouldn't depend on someone else's licensing decisions.

I'm Aaron Salisbury, the founder: a Christian, husband, father of three, Army veteran, and software engineer.
